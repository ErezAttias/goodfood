Eat Tel Aviv. אוכלים בתל אביב

No search, no ratings, no endless options. Just the short list of places we take our own friends to. Each place has its Hebrew name, so you can show it to the taxi driver.

Before you go

Book ahead
Thursday and Friday nights fill up. Most places book through Ontopo or Tabit, both in English.
The weekend is Friday–Saturday
Shabbat runs from Friday sunset to Saturday night. Many places stay open; kosher ones close.
Tip 12–15%
Service is usually not included. Tell the server the total you want charged, tip included.
Dinner is late
8:30–9pm is normal. Weekday "business lunch" menus are a good deal.
Getting around
Gett or Yango for taxis, Wolt for delivery to the hotel. Tap water is fine to drink.
Cards everywhere
Apple Pay and Google Pay work almost everywhere, including most street stalls.
