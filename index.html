Where to eat in Tel Aviv 🥙

Welcome to Israel! These are the places your Israeli colleagues actually go to. Filter by cuisine, budget or occasion, then tap through to Instagram, Maps or booking.

Good to know before you go
  • Book ahead. Popular places fill up, especially Thursday and Friday nights. Most restaurants use Ontopo or Tabit for online booking — no Hebrew needed.
  • The weekend is Friday–Saturday. Shabbat runs Friday sunset to Saturday night. Many places stay open, but kosher restaurants close, and some close Friday afternoon.
  • Tipping: 12–15% is standard at sit-down restaurants and is usually not included in the bill. Tell the server the total you want to charge, including the tip.
  • Cards are accepted almost everywhere; Apple Pay / Google Pay work widely.
  • Dinner runs late. 8:30–9:00pm is a normal dinner time. Lunch deals ("business lunch") are common on weekdays.
  • Getting around: use Gett or Yango for taxis. For delivery to your hotel, use Wolt.
  • Tap water is safe to drink.
